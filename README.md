## What is Detta?

**Detta is self-help debt-resolution software.** It gives consumers the tools, letter templates, phone scripts, calculators, and step-by-step guidance to work through their own **unsecured** debts — credit cards, medical bills, personal loans, and accounts in collections — by contacting creditors **directly**.

You stay in control of your own money the entire time. **There is no escrow and no middleman account** — Detta never holds, moves, or manages your funds.

Detta is **not** a debt-settlement company, a debt-relief or debt-adjustment service, a credit-counseling agency, or a law firm, and it does **not** negotiate with creditors on your behalf. It is software that helps you do the work yourself.

> Detta is in a **pre-launch waitlist** phase. This repository contains the public waitlist website only.
