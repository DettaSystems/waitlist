_Built on fact-checked deep research (2026-06-26; 24 sources, 25 claims adversarially

## The one reframe that changes the plan

**Signups and AI citations do not come from detta.us. They come from the outposts.**
The strongest verified finding: AI engines cite **third-party platforms, not brand
sites** (aggregate AI citations rank Reddit > YouTube > LinkedIn > Wikipedia > Forbes;
~82-89% of AI citations are earned/third-party; brand *mentions* correlate with AI
Overview visibility far more than backlinks). So the engine is **home base + outposts**:
cornerstone content is the home base; the newsletter and the platforms AI actually
cites are where discovery, audience, and signups happen.

**This also means we can start NOW, without the Astro content hub.** A cornerstone can
launch as a newsletter issue + a LinkedIn post + a YouTube explainer + genuine Reddit
participation before a blog page exists. The hub (and its SEO payoff) is the *later*
home for these pieces, not a blocker to starting distribution.

## What the evidence actually supports (and what it killed)

**Confirmed (plan on these):**
- AI citation = presence on Reddit / YouTube / LinkedIn, matched to engine: ChatGPT
  favors Wikipedia/Reddit/Forbes; Perplexity favors Reddit/LinkedIn/G2; Google leans
  Facebook/Yelp/YouTube.
- Newsletter cadence: **weekly** is the pragmatic floor (click rates rise to a
  twice-weekly peak of 5.31%; monthly-or-less only 3.74%).
- **A welcome/triggered email sequence is the single highest-ROI asset** (2-3x the
  click-through of broadcasts in financial services). Build it first.
- Finance newsletters convert well only at elite execution (top-decile 20% free-to-paid
  vs 0.62% median). So the newsletter's early job is **signup capture + audience
  compounding, not revenue.**
- Early newsletter growth comes from **mid-sized peer cross-recommendations**, not big
  names (a verified 12:1 outflow from "major" recommenders). beehiiv Boosts is *paid*.
- FTC Section 5 governs all social/finfluencer content: truthful, substantiated,
  **typical-not-exceptional** results, disclosed connections ($3M Warrior Trading
  penalty). Bake into every piece.

**Killed (do NOT plan around):** quiz/calculator lead magnets converting 40-51%
(those are warm-audience outliers; model cold traffic at **low-to-mid single digits**
and measure); "Reddit is #1 everywhere"; "structured data = 3-5x more citations";
"frequency lowers unsubscribes"; specific Substack growth trajectories. **All concrete
follower/signup trajectory numbers were refuted, so 90-day targets are hypotheses to
validate, not promises.**

## (b) Newsletter concept

- **Name (pick one):** *The Free & Clear* (recommended) · *Even Keel* · *The Payoff*.
  Calm, self-help, never "settlement."
- **Platform:** **beehiiv** (custom domain `newsletter.detta.us`, owns the list, free
  Recommendations network + native referral program). Substack is the alternative if
  we want its recommendation network as the primary growth lever.
- **The list IS the waitlist** - one signup, one welcome sequence, one weekly send.
- **Cadence:** weekly.
- **Voice:** plain-English, calm, attorney-credible but warm; "self-help software, not
  legal advice" footer; no hype, no outcome talk.
- **Signature recurring segment:** **"One right you have"** - a 120-word weekly
  FDCPA/FCRA consumer-right explainer from the attorney founders. Educational,
  endlessly repeatable, screenshot-shareable, and exactly the quotable/credentialed
  content AI engines cite.

## (d) Weekly repurposing workflow (one cornerstone → many)

Batch on one day:
1. **Cornerstone** drafted + attorney-reviewed (home base).
2. → **Newsletter issue** (cornerstone edited for email + "One right you have").
3. → **YouTube** explainer (founder on camera) + a **Short**.
4. → **LinkedIn** post (the single sharpest insight, founder voice).
5. → **3-5 short clips / carousels** for IG/TikTok.
6. → **X/Threads** thread.
7. → **Reddit**: answer 2-3 genuinely relevant live threads that week (value first;
   link only where the sub permits and only when truly useful).

## (e) Lead-magnet + CTA path to a waitlist signup

- **Magnets (must be self-help, never the paid generators):** the **live payoff
  calculator and settlement planner already on detta.us**, plus a downloadable
  **"Debt-collector call checklist"** and **"Know-your-rights one-pager."**
- **CTA everywhere:** "Join the waitlist for early access + *The Free & Clear* weekly."
- **Welcome sequence (build FIRST - highest ROI):** (1) attorney-founder intro + what
  Detta is and is not; (2) the rights one-pager; (3) the "do I need a settlement
  company?" reframe; (4) what's coming + how early access works.
- **Conversion expectation:** model cold-traffic opt-ins at **low-to-mid single
  digits**, A/B test, do not plan around the killed 30-50% figures.

## (f) Weekly cadence + the only metrics that matter now

- **Cadence:** 1 cornerstone + 1 newsletter + the repurpose set, weekly; plus light,
  genuine daily community presence.
- **Metrics (ignore vanity):**
  1. **Net new waitlist signups / week, by source** (north star).
  2. **Newsletter health** - click rate (toward ~5%), open rate (~33% baseline), list
     growth, welcome-sequence CTR.
  3. **Entity / AI-citation leading indicators** - branded-search impressions (Search
     Console) + a monthly manual check: does any engine cite Detta for the money query
     ("resolve my own debt without a settlement company")?
- **Honest expectation:** 90 days builds the foundation and the *first* compounding
  signals, not a signup flood. AI-citation traction is a 12-18 month game.

## Owner decisions this plan is gated on

1. **Named attorney author** (real name + bar/state) for bylines + welcome-sequence
   intro. This is THE unlock - without it, YMYL content underperforms and AI won't
   trust it. Highest-priority decision.
2. **Who is on camera / posting** (founder-as-face is ideal for E-E-A-T).
3. **Newsletter platform:** approve beehiiv (recommended) vs Substack.
4. **Capacity:** this is ~1 cornerstone/week of attorney-reviewed output. Confirm who
   writes and who reviews.
