Detta is **self-help debt-resolution software**. It gives consumers the tools,
letter templates, phone scripts, calculators, and step-by-step guidance to
resolve their own **unsecured** debts — credit cards, medical bills, personal
loans, and accounts in collections — by contacting creditors **directly**.

You stay in control of your own money. There is no escrow and no middleman —
Detta never holds or moves your funds.

Detta is **not** a debt-settlement company, a debt-relief or debt-adjustment
service, a credit-counseling agency, or a law firm.

> Currently in a **pre-launch waitlist** phase → **[detta.us](https://www.detta.us/)**

<sub>Self-help software. Not legal advice. Not an offer of securities.</sub>
