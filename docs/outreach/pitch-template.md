# Roundup outreach — pitch template

> Goal: get Detta **honestly** included in a third-party "best debt tools" / "DIY debt" roundup, in the **self-help / DIY** category. White-hat only. See [brand-kit.md](brand-kit.md) for the exact language and [roundup-target-list.md](roundup-target-list.md) for who to pitch.

## The rules (read before sending)

1. **One author at a time.** Personalize every email. No mass/BCC sends.
2. **Lead with substance, not a feature dump.** Editors in finance (a YMYL topic) reject partnership-flavored pitches; they respond to accuracy and genuine value.
3. **State the category yourself, every time** — Detta is self-help *software*, not a settlement service. Ask to be listed in the DIY/self-help category.
4. **Disclose any material connection.** A free press login is fine *if* you say it's offered with no strings and not conditioned on inclusion.
5. **Never** offer payment, affiliate revenue, or sponsorship for a placement.
6. **Log every pitch** in [pitch-log.md](pitch-log.md) — date, who, what you offered, exact disclaimer sent.

## Email template

**Subject:** `Quick note on your [ARTICLE TITLE] — a self-help (DIY) option`

```
Hi [FIRST NAME],

I read your piece, "[ARTICLE TITLE]" — [one genuine, specific sentence
about something in it, e.g. "your point that snowball wins on follow-through
even when avalanche wins on math is the clearest version of that I've seen"].

I'm [YOUR NAME], [role] at Detta. Detta is self-help software for people who
want to resolve their own unsecured debt — they contact creditors directly,
and we provide the templates, scripts, calculators, and step-by-step guidance.
There's no escrow and we never hold anyone's money. To be clear about the
category: Detta is NOT a debt-settlement, debt-relief, or credit-counseling
service, and not a law firm — it's a DIY tool the person operates themselves.

If it's useful for a future update, I'm happy to [offer ONE: give you a free
press login to evaluate it / fact-check the "DIY vs. hiring a company" section
of your article]. No payment, sponsorship, or affiliate arrangement is involved
or offered — and the login isn't conditioned on being listed.

If you do consider Detta, the accurate placement is the self-help / DIY-tools
category, not among settlement companies.

Either way, thanks for writing something genuinely useful on this.

[YOUR NAME]
[email] · https://www.detta.us/
```

## Follow-up (once, after ~7–10 days, only if no reply)

```
Hi [FIRST NAME] — just floating this back up in case it's useful for a future
update to "[ARTICLE TITLE]". No worries if it's not a fit. Thanks again.
[YOUR NAME]
```

## If a list mislabels Detta (important)

If an article lists Detta as a "debt settlement" or "debt relief" service, send a short, friendly correction:

```
Thanks so much for including Detta! One small accuracy fix: Detta is self-help
software (a DIY tool people operate themselves), not a debt-settlement or
debt-relief service — we never negotiate for users or hold their money. Could
the entry be moved to the self-help / DIY category and the wording adjusted?
Happy to provide a one-line description that's accurate. Really appreciate it.
```

```
guidance to resolve their own unsecured debt by contacting creditors directly.
No escrow, no middleman. (Self-help tool; not a debt-settlement, debt-relief,

```
