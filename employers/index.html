The gap

Why debt, and why now

Debt stress follows people to work: the collector who calls during a shift, the wage garnishment that lands on payroll's desk, the employee too anxious to focus. Most benefits stacks have nothing for that moment. Detta gives employees the tools to handle it themselves: understand their rights, generate the letters, make a plan, and track it through, all in plain English.

✓

Self-help, not settlement

Employees resolve debt themselves with guided tools. Detta never holds their money and never contacts their creditors. Not a law firm, debt-settlement company, or credit-counseling agency.

§

Attorney-reviewed guidance

Built by founders who practice consumer-protection law. Every guide and template is reviewed for accuracy, with sources employees can check.

♥

Made for the hard moments

Validation letters, negotiation planning, statute-of-limitations awareness, lawsuit deadlines: the acute problems, not just budgeting basics.

Never.

The privacy line is absolute

Employers never see any employee's debts, documents, balances, or activity. Ever. Financial stress is private, and a benefit only works if your people trust it. That is a design decision, not a policy preference.
